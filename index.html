Luna and the
Lantern Bugs

a bedtime story

For every little one who's afraid of the dark —
the fireflies are on your side.

Luna didn't like bedtime. The dark felt too big, and too quiet, and too full of maybes.

1

Then, one night, a tiny light blinked outside her window. Then another. And another.

2

"We're lantern bugs," they hummed. "We carry a little bit of daylight, just for nights like this."

3

Luna smiled, closed her eyes, and let the tiny lights carry her off to sleep.

4
